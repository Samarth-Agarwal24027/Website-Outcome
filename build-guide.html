How to Build a PC

Follow these simple steps to safely assemble your own computer.

Building Steps

Step 1

Install the CPU onto the motherboard and lock it into place.

Step 2

Install the RAM and storage drive (SSD or HDD).

Step 3

Mount the motherboard securely inside the PC case.

Step 4

Install the graphics card if your build includes one.

Step 5

Connect the power supply cables to every required component.

Step 6

Check all connections, power on the computer, and install the operating system.
