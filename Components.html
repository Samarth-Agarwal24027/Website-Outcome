Learn More About Each Component

Central Processing Unit (CPU)

The CPU is often called the brain of the computer because it processes instructions and performs calculations. It affects how quickly programs run and how well your computer handles multitasking.

Graphics Processing Unit (GPU)

The GPU creates the images displayed on your monitor. A dedicated graphics card improves gaming, video editing, 3D modelling, and other graphics-intensive tasks.

Random Access Memory (RAM)

RAM stores data that your computer is currently using. More RAM allows you to run multiple programs smoothly and improves overall system responsiveness.

Storage (SSD & HDD)

Storage keeps your operating system, applications, and personal files. SSDs are much faster than HDDs, while HDDs usually provide more storage capacity at a lower cost.

Motherboard

The motherboard is the main circuit board that connects every component together. It allows the CPU, RAM, storage, graphics card, and other hardware to communicate.

Power Supply Unit (PSU)

The PSU converts electricity from the wall into power that your computer can use. Choosing a reliable power supply helps keep your system stable and protects your components.

PC Case

The PC case holds and protects all of the internal hardware. A well-designed case provides good airflow, cable management, and room for future upgrades.

CPU Cooler

The CPU cooler removes heat from the processor to prevent overheating. Keeping the CPU cool improves performance and extends the lifespan of the computer.

Thermal Compound

Thermal compound is applied between the CPU and the cooler. It improves heat transfer by filling tiny gaps between the two surfaces, helping the processor stay cooler.
