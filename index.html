What is a PC?

A Personal Computer (PC) is a versatile digital device designed to run operating systems and software so you can study, work, game, browse the internet, or create documents. It integrates internal hardware like a CPU (processor) to handle calculations, a motherboard to connect all parts, RAM for temporary memory, and an SSD/HDD for storage. PCs also use graphics cards (GPUs) for visuals, a PSU for power, and input/output peripherals like keyboards, mice, and monitors. The most popular formats are stationary desktops and portable laptops used daily across homes, schools, and offices.

Building your perfect PC

Choose the right components

Selecting compatible components is the first step in building a reliable PC. Choosing the right CPU, GPU, motherboard, RAM, and storage ensures your computer performs efficiently and meets your needs.

Plan Your Budget

Decide how much you want to spend before buying parts. Investing more in the components that matter most for your needs helps you build a balanced and cost-effective computer.

Essential PC Components

Every PC is built from several important components. Each part has a specific role that helps your computer run smoothly and efficiently.

Central Processing Unit (CPU)

Learn how the processor handles calculations and powers your computer.

Graphics Processing Unit (GPU)

Discover how graphics cards improve gaming, video editing, and 3D rendering.

Random Access Memory (RAM)

Understand how memory affects multitasking and overall system performance.

Solid State Drive (SSD) & Hard Disk Drive (HDD)

Store your operating system, games, applications, and files with fast SSDs or high-capacity HDDs.

Motherboard

The main circuit board that connects and allows all computer components to communicate.

Power Supply Unit (PSU)

Supplies safe and reliable power to every component in your computer.

PC Case

Houses and protects all PC components while providing airflow and cable management.

CPU Cooler

Removes heat from the processor to keep it cool and maintain reliable performance.

Thermal Compound

Improves heat transfer between the CPU and cooler for more efficient cooling.
