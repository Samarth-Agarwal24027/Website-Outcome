PC Building Guide

What is a PC?

A Personal Computer (PC) is a versatile digital device designed to run operating systems and software so you can study, work, game, browse the internet, or create documents. It integrates internal hardware like a CPU (processor) to handle calculations, a motherboard to connect all parts, RAM for temporary memory, and an SSD/HDD for storage. PCs also use graphics cards (GPUs) for visuals, a PSU for power, and input/output peripherals like keyboards, mice, and monitors. The most popular formats are stationary desktops and portable laptops used daily across homes, schools, and offices.

Building your perfect PC

Choose the right components

Selecting compatible components is the first step in building a reliable PC. Choosing the right CPU, GPU, motherboard, RAM, and storage ensures your computer performs efficiently and meets your needs.

Decide how much you want to spend before buying parts. Investing more in the components that matter most for your needs helps you build a balanced and cost-effective computer.
